At DataNurse, we are committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of all patient data collected through our platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, safeguard, retain, and delete information when you use the DataNurse mobile application or admin portal.

1. Introduction

DataNurse ("we", "our", or "the platform") is a healthcare data management application operated by Infotech, Pakistan. We provide digital tools for heart care surgeons and healthcare professionals to record, manage, and analyze patient surgical data for research and quality-improvement purposes.

DataNurse does not process payments and does not offer consumer-facing accounts to patients. All users of the platform are verified, adult healthcare professionals or administrators.

By using DataNurse - whether via our Android mobile application or our web-based admin portal - you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the platform.

2. Information We Collect

Patient Data

Healthcare professionals using DataNurse enter the following types of patient information:

  • Patient demographics (name, age, gender, date of birth, CNIC/MRN)
  • Medical history and diagnosis information
  • Surgical procedure details (procedure type, bypass time, clamp time)
  • Pre-operative and post-operative clinical data
  • Recovery status and follow-up notes

User Account Data

For registered healthcare professionals and administrators, we collect:

  • Name, email address, and professional designation
  • Login credentials (passwords are stored as salted hashes - never in plain text)
  • Role and permission assignments
  • Activity logs for audit purposes

Technical Data

We automatically collect certain technical information including device type, IP address, browser/app version, and usage patterns to maintain and improve the platform.

We collect Personal and Sensitive Information (PII) including but not limited to Name, Email, and Patient Medical Records, solely for the purposes of clinical research and surgical tracking.

3. How We Use Your Data

Data collected through DataNurse is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Providing and operating the DataNurse platform
  • Patient care management and surgical outcome tracking
  • Generating reports and analytics for healthcare quality improvement and geographic research
  • User authentication and access control
  • Platform maintenance, debugging, and security monitoring
  • Compliance with applicable Pakistani healthcare regulations

We do not sell, rent, or share patient or user data with third parties for commercial, advertising, or marketing purposes.

4. Data Storage & Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect all data stored on our platform:

  • Encryption in transit: All data transmitted between the app/portal and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.2+
  • Encryption at rest: Sensitive data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption
  • Access controls: Role-based access ensures users can only access data relevant to their responsibilities
  • Authentication: JWT-based authentication with token expiry and refresh mechanisms
  • Audit logging: All data access and modification events are logged for security review

While we implement strong security measures, no system is completely immune to breaches. We encourage users to maintain strong, unique passwords and report any suspicious activity immediately to info@datanurse.io.

5. Data Sharing

Patient information is accessible only to verified healthcare professionals at the specific institution where the patient is registered. We do not share data across institutions without explicit consent.

We may share data in the following limited circumstances:

  • With your hospital's DataNurse administrator as part of normal platform operation
  • With our technical service providers (servers, hosting) under strict confidentiality agreements
  • When required by Pakistani law, a court order, or a regulatory authority
  • With your explicit written consent for research or quality-improvement purposes

6. Data Retention

Patient Medical Records

Patient medical records are retained in accordance with your institution's data retention policies and applicable Pakistani healthcare regulations. In the absence of a specific institutional policy, we retain patient records for a minimum of 7 years from the date of last interaction, as required by healthcare compliance standards.

User Account Data

User account information is retained while accounts remain active and operational. Upon a verified account closure or deactivation request submitted to privacy@datanurse.io, personal information that is not subject to a legal, medical, audit, or regulatory hold will be deleted or anonymized within 30 days.

Technical & Log Data

System logs, access records, and technical data are retained for a maximum of 12 months and then permanently deleted, unless a specific security or legal investigation requires extended retention.

Note: Certain medical, audit, security, and operational records may be retained longer where required by healthcare regulations, institutional policies, fraud prevention requirements, or applicable Pakistani law - even after a deletion request is received.

7. Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data from the DataNurse platform. We provide a clear and straightforward process for all data deletion requests.

Users can request the deletion of their account and all associated personal data. Upon a verified request, we will remove the user's account information from our active databases within 30 days.

How to request deletion of your data:

  • Send an email to privacy@datanurse.io with the subject line "Data Deletion Request"
  • Include your full name, registered email address, and professional designation
  • Specify whether you want your account closed, specific records corrected, or all personal data deleted

We will acknowledge your request within 5 business days and complete the deletion or provide a detailed response within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Upon a verified deletion request, the following data will be permanently removed or anonymized:

  • Your user account credentials (email, password hash, profile details)
  • Personal identifiers linked to your account activity
  • Device and login session records associated with your account

What cannot be deleted immediately

Certain data may be retained even after a deletion request, where required by applicable law or institutional policy:

  • Patient medical records - retained for the minimum period required by Pakistani healthcare regulations (typically 7 years) regardless of who entered them
  • Audit logs - required for legal compliance and institutional accountability
  • Data subject to an active legal hold - retained until the legal matter is resolved

When deletion is not immediately possible due to the above, we will clearly communicate the reason and the expected timeline to you in writing.

Anonymous / De-identified data

Aggregated, de-identified, or anonymized research data that cannot be traced back to any individual is not subject to deletion requests, as it no longer constitutes personal data under applicable privacy frameworks.

8. Your Rights

As a user or data subject, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data (see Section 7 for the full process and applicable exceptions)
  • Restriction: Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances
  • Objection: Object to processing of your personal data in certain circumstances

To exercise any of the above rights, contact our Privacy Team at privacy@datanurse.io. We review and respond to all verified requests within 30 days, subject to applicable legal, medical, and regulatory retention requirements.

9. Children's Privacy

DataNurse is a healthcare professional platform and is not directed at or intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. All platform users must be adults and verified healthcare professionals or administrators.

While our platform manages data about patients (children requiring cardiac surgery), all users of the platform must be adults and verified healthcare professionals.

Patient data is handled with the highest level of care and confidentiality, with access strictly controlled to authorized medical personnel at the relevant institution.

When we make material changes, we will notify users via email and in-app notification at least 14 days before the changes take effect.

Your continued use of DataNurse after the effective date of any changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
