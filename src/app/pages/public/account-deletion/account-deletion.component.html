Healthcare Data & Account Management

DataNurse is a healthcare data management platform used by authorized medical professionals and institutions. Due to medical, legal, operational, and regulatory requirements, certain records and account-related information may be retained and managed in accordance with institutional healthcare policies and applicable Pakistani laws.

Account Access Requests

Users may contact the DataNurse support team regarding account-related concerns or access restrictions.

1

Contact Support

Users may contact the DataNurse support team using their registered email address and institution details.

2

Verification Process

Our team may verify the request with the associated healthcare institution or administrator before processing.

3

Access Restriction or Deactivation

Where applicable, user access to the platform may be restricted, suspended, or deactivated.

4

Institutional Record Retention

Medical, audit, and operational records may continue to be retained in accordance with institutional policies, healthcare regulations, security requirements, and applicable law.

For account-related inquiries, contact us at info@datanurse.io.

How Data Is Managed

DataNurse follows healthcare-focused operational and security practices.

Security

Protected Access

Access to medical and institutional data is restricted to authorized healthcare professionals and administrators.

Compliance

Institutional Oversight

Data handling practices may be governed by hospital administration policies and applicable healthcare regulations.

Audit & Security

Operational Record Retention

Certain records, logs, and security-related information may be retained for operational integrity and compliance purposes.

Healthcare Records

Medical Data Preservation

Patient-related medical information may be preserved according to institutional healthcare retention requirements and applicable law.

Data Retention Notice

DataNurse may retain certain records and information where required for:

Medical and institutional record-keeping requirements
Security monitoring, audit logging, and fraud prevention
Applicable Pakistani healthcare, operational, or legal compliance obligations

For institution-specific data concerns, please contact your hospital administrator or DataNurse support team.
