Account Management

Account Deletion

Learn how to request deletion of your DataNurse account and what happens to your data.

How Account Deletion Works

Account deletion requests are submitted through the DataNurse mobile app. Once submitted, our team processes the request within 3 business days. The steps below explain the full process.

How to Submit a Request

Follow these steps inside the DataNurse mobile app:

1

Open the App

Log in to the DataNurse mobile app using your registered credentials.

2

Go to Profile Settings

Tap your profile icon and navigate to Settings → Account.

3

Request Account Deletion

Tap "Delete My Account", select a reason, and confirm your request.

4

Confirmation Email

You will receive a confirmation email with a reference ticket ID within a few minutes.

Can't access the app? Email us at info@datanurse.io with your registered email and hospital name and we'll process your request manually.

What Happens After

Here's how we process your request step by step:

Step 1 · Immediate

Request Received

Your deletion request is logged and you receive a confirmation email with a ticket reference.

Step 2 · 1–3 Business Days

Identity Verification

Our team verifies your identity and may contact your hospital administrator to confirm the request.

Step 3 · Upon Approval

Account Deactivated

Your login access is immediately revoked. You will no longer be able to sign in to the app or portal.

Step 4 · Within 90 Days

Data Permanently Deleted

Your personal account data (name, email, credentials) is permanently and irreversibly deleted from our systems.

Data We May Retain After Deletion

Even after your account is deleted, certain data may be retained as required by Pakistani healthcare regulations and medical record-keeping laws:

Patient medical records you entered (retained by the institution)
Audit logs required for clinical data integrity
Records required for legal or regulatory compliance

For questions about institutional data, contact your hospital's DataNurse administrator.
