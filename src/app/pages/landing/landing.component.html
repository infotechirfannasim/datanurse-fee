About DataNurse

Built for
Health Care Heroes

DataNurse is a purpose-built mobile and web application designed for cardiologists surgeons. Patient with congenital heart defects require specialized, life-saving surgeries — and every detail of their care matters.

Our platform bridges the gap between mobile data collection at the bedside and centralized management through an admin portal. Doctors can record patient information, surgical procedures, pre- and post-operative data, and track recovery — all in real time.

With DataNurse, hospitals and clinics gain the power of structured healthcare data, enabling better decision-making, audit trails, and improved patient outcomes

Mission: To digitize and standardize heart care surgical records, enabling data-driven improvements in Patient's heart care.

Mobile App

Capture patient data at the point of care using our intuitive Android application.

Admin Portal

Manage doctors, patients, cases and reports from a powerful web dashboard.

Structured Data

Standardized forms ensure consistent, high-quality data across all hospitals.

Focused

Designed for the specific workflows of surgery teams.

App Features

Everything You Need to
Manage Patient's Heart Care Cases

Patient Registration

Register patients with complete demographics, medical history, and diagnosis information quickly and accurately.

Surgical Case Records

Document complete surgical details including procedure type, bypass time, aortic clamp time, and intraoperative findings.

Doctor Management

Maintain profiles of surgeons and care teams. Track each doctor's surgical history and outcomes.

Dashboard Analytics

Visual dashboards show active cases, recovery rates, surgical outcomes, and hospital statistics at a glance.

Pre & Post-op Tracking

Record pre-operative assessments and post-operative monitoring including vitals and recovery milestones.

Role-Based Access

Granular permissions ensure doctors see only what they need. Admins control full access securely.

Secure Authentication

JWT-based login with forgot password and reset flows. Hospital data protected and accessible only to authorized users.

Reports & Export

Generate comprehensive surgical reports and data exports for research and quality improvement.

Offline-Ready Mobile App

The Android app works in low-connectivity environments — data syncs automatically when connection is restored.
